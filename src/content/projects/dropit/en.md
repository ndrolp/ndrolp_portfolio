## The goal

Create a judgment-free experience that helps people reduce tobacco use at their own pace. DropIt turns habits, schedules, and triggers into an easy-to-follow daily plan.

## The solution

DropIt is a local-first Flutter app. It lets people create a profile, plan their consumption, log cigarettes and cravings, and review progress, estimated savings, and personal patterns.

## Features

- Daily plans adapted to habits, meals, and goals.
- Cigarette, craving, and trigger logging.
- Progress, adherence, and estimated-savings insights.
- Local reminders that support the plan.
- JSON backup export and import.
- Data stored only on the device.
